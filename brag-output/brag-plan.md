## Planning rubric
1. App: controlled USB-C hub procurement with server-owned policy and explicit human checkout approval.
2. Strongest claim: Equip your team. Stay in control.
3. Visual hook: oversized amber CONTROL against the product’s obsidian ledger palette.
4. Actual UI: fixture quote, policy checks, blocked checkout, and readable Decision Passport from evidence/command-ui.
5. Duration: exactly 60 seconds, as requested; overrides the skill’s normal short-video default.
6. Tone: polished; a confident procurement command-center demonstration.
7. Audio: warm, steady vol-12 music at 0.30 with restrained click accents and final fade; no narration requested.
8. Caption: SpendPilot turns one equipment request into an inspectable quote, server-enforced spending checks, and a decision you approve. This buildathon demo uses simulated fixture data; no live payment is claimed.
9. Flow: confirm scope → inspect quote and policy → explicit approval step, plus a blocked request and persisted evidence.

- 22–31 Check policy: real policy crop plus trusted item, merchant and budget callouts. Three callouts enter one by one and hold. “11 / 11” is fixture evidence only.
- 31–39 Human approval: source-derived checkout control; pointer taps to show the approval handoff concept. Prominent “Human action required”; no provider page or completed payment is simulated.
- 39–47 Block bad requests: real blocked UI crop; “Two hubs? Blocked.” and “No checkout created” demonstrate out-of-scope rejection.
- 47–55 Keep the evidence: readable real Decision Passport crop; scope, quote and policy events remain source-labelled.
- 55–60 Close: SpendPilot. “One request. Clear limits. Your approval.” End with “Reap × 65labs sandbox demo” and no unverified launch claim.

Every scene carries “SIMULATED DEMO • NO LIVE PAYMENT”. Screenshot crops are real prior local fixture evidence; motion and pointer actions are illustrative, not a live recording. No checkout is initiated to make this video.
